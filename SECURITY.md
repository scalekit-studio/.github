# Security policy

## Report a vulnerability

Email `security@scalekit.studio` before public disclosure. If private vulnerability
reporting is enabled on the affected repository, a private GitHub security advisory is
also acceptable.

Include:

- Affected repository, service, version, commit, or URL.
- Reproduction steps or a minimal proof of concept.
- Expected and observed behavior.
- Potential impact and required preconditions.
- Any evidence that data or credentials were accessed.
- A safe way to contact you for follow-up.

Do not include secrets or personal data beyond what is necessary to demonstrate the
problem. Do not test against accounts, data, or infrastructure you do not own or have
explicit permission to use.

We aim to acknowledge a report within two business days, investigate it promptly, and
coordinate remediation and disclosure with the reporter. Good-faith research that stays
within these boundaries will not be pursued as abuse.

## Scope

Security reports include authentication or authorization failures, cross-tenant access,
credential exposure, injection, unsafe execution, sensitive-data leakage, dependency
compromise, and infrastructure access-control defects.

General defects, availability questions, account help, and feature requests belong in
the support process described in [`SUPPORT.md`](SUPPORT.md).

The current public policy is also available at
[`scalekit.studio/security`](https://scalekit.studio/security).
